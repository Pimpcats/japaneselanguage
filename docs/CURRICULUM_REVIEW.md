# はなそう (Hanasou) — Full Curriculum for Review

A speaking-first Japanese course: the learner sees the **English**, says it out loud in **Japanese**, then reveals the answer and self-grades into a light spaced-repetition system.

**Reviewer — please flag anything off:** unnatural phrasing, inconsistent or wrong politeness level, particle errors, incorrect readings (romaji / furigana), or vocabulary that does not fit the lesson's grammar point. Each lesson teaches exactly ONE grammar point, and its sentences should only recombine that point with its listed vocab.

**Scope:** 84 lessons · 560 vocab entries · 503 sentences, across 8 levels (Level 0 First Sounds → Level 7 Master).

Furigana readings for kanji are shown in parentheses, e.g. 会議（かいぎ）.

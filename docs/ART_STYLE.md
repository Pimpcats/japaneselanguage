# はなそう — ART STYLE BIBLE (the one cohesive look)

The whole app matches ONE style, defined by these reference sheets (owner-approved):
**O1 food · O4 town/transport · O7 misc · Mochiko.** Objects and Mochiko already
ARE this style — do not redo them. Regenerate the **covers** to match.

## THE STYLE
Clean, confident **hand-inked outlines** — medium-to-bold, consistent line
weight, genuinely hand-drawn (not sketchy, not thin vector). **Refined
colouring:** soft cel shading with gentle watercolour/gouache gradients *inside*
each form; a **muted, natural, slightly desaturated palette** — warm and
elegant, never neon, never washed-out pastel. **Real detail** — surface texture,
individual hair strokes, patterns, small highlights. **Clean, simple
backgrounds:** a single soft flat colour, nothing busy.

### AVOID (these break cohesion)
- **NO mottled / cloudy / grid-like watercolour WASH in the background**, and no
  stamped all-over paper texture — this is the #1 AI tell. Backgrounds stay flat
  and clean.
- No flat clip-art / vector look (keep the hand-inked line + interior shading).
- No neon or candy colours; keep it muted and natural.

### CONTENT RULES
Open books show lines of text. Documents/reports/signs show writing. A notepad
may be blank only if the context fits. Menus/tickets carry a label.

# Living City — art still to draw

Every pop-up in the Living City book is chosen by hand to match its sentence
(`living-city/scenes.js`). These pictures are referenced but not drawn yet — until
they exist, those pages simply show fewer pop-ups (never a wrong one).

**How to add one:** paste the prompt into ChatGPT, cut the green-screen result with
`python3 tools/cut_sheet.py <image> 1x1 <name> assets/story` (objects), or save a
backdrop as `assets/story/<name>.jpg`, then push. It appears automatically.

Generated by `tools/build_living_city_scenes.mjs` — 39 objects, 2 postcards.
